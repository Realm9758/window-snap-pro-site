# Redock

## What it is

A macOS menu bar utility that remembers where your windows belong and puts them
back. Formerly Window Snap Pro; renamed to name the moment it earns its keep.

Requires macOS 13 or later. Distributed outside the App Store as a direct
download. Not sandboxed, because moving other apps' windows requires
Accessibility access.

## The one thing it does that others don't

Save the position of every open window across every display, then restore it.
Automatically, when the same monitor arrangement is reconnected.

Rectangle, Magnet, Raycast and macOS's own tiling all snap windows. **None of
them save or restore layouts.** Moom does, and is widely called dated. That gap
is the entire reason this product exists, and the only thing worth leading with.

The daily scene: you dock your laptop at a desk, and everything you had open
scatters. Redock puts it back.

## Audience

Mac users with an external display who dock and undock. Developers, designers,
anyone who works from more than one desk. They already know what a window
manager is and many already use a free one, so the page has to answer "why pay"
in the first screen.

## Voice

Plain, specific, quiet. State what happens, not how it feels.

Banned outright: em dashes. Superlatives the product hasn't earned ("fastest",
"most intuitive", "seamless", "buttery smooth", "effortless"). Filler like
"second nature" or "your workflow, your rules". Fake urgency and invented
social proof: there are no users to quote yet, and inventing them is fraud.

Say "windows go back where they were", not "reclaim your workspace".
